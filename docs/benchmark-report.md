### Score Formula
PPL weighted 70%, TPS weighted 30% — output quality matters more than speed.
Each scored 0–1 against a percentile cap (90th TPS, 10th warm PPL) then scaled
to 0–100. Percentile caps prevent extreme outliers from compressing the scale.
TPS = `min(1, cold_tps * warm_tps / cap_90)`
PPL = `cap_10 / warm_ppl` — warm (hot) PPL only. A model whose PPL rises with
  context is penalized by its higher absolute warm value.
Final = `(0.3 * TPS + 0.7 * PPL) * 100`
